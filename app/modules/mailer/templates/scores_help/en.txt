Understand our scores
- Career: How well the offer fits your profile and career path, independent of the recruiter's automated filters.
- ATS: How well your CV matches the applicant tracking systems (ATS) recruiters use to filter CVs.
- ATS Potential: The highest ATS score you could reach for this offer by optimizing your CV.

- Very strong fit: ATS Potential of 95 or more
- Strong fit: ATS Potential of 80 to 94
